What we heard

The brief

A personal mark for the portfolio of a systems developer. It should feel playful and handmade but still technical, match the sticker look of the site, and stay readable as a 16 px favicon.

playfulhandmadetechnicalbold

Success: unmistakably NW, matches the site's tiles, readable at 16 px, works in one colour, different from the usual terminal-icon developer logos.

Concept A

Tiles

Your initials on two tilted letter tiles, the same tiles that drop in on the site.

  • Ties the logo directly to the site's hero and Build/Break/Rebuild tiles
  • Reads as NW at a glance and at 16 px, with a one-letter favicon for the smallest sizes
  • Hard shadow and thick outline are the site's sticker language, so it reproduces as flat shapes
